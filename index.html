Department of Dravyaguna
Tvak
Botanical Name :

  • Cinnamomum zeylanica Blume

    • Family Name :

  • Lauraceae

    • Local Name :

  • Dalchini chakke

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi –Dalchini

  • English – Cinnamon

    • Synonyms :

  • Dārucinī

  • Utkata


    • Habitat :

  • Tvak plant is cultivated in Western and Southern India


    • External Morphology :

  • Leaves : Opposite,or sub opposite,hard and coriaceous,7.5 - 20 cm long 4 - 7.5 cm wide,ovate or ovate lanceolate,subacute

  • Flowers : Numerous,pedicels are long Perianth 5 – 6 mm long

  • Fruit : 1 – 1.5 cm long,oblong or ovoid – oblong,minutely apiculate,dry or slightly fleshy,dark purple


    • Useful Parts :

  • Twak (Bark)


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa – katu Tikta Madhura

  • Guna –Laghu Tiksna

  • Vipaka - Katu

  • Virya –Usna


    • Pharmalogical Actions :

  • Carminative

  • Antispasmodic


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Kasa : Powder of Twak,Ela,Pippali,Ksira and Sugar mixed in successively double quantity taken with honey and ghee in case of Kasa.It is also useful in Asthma,Chest pain and to improve voice

  • Paittika Siroroga: Paste of Twak,Patra and Sugar should be mixed with rice water and used as a Avapida nasya it is efficacious in headache originated by Pitta dosa


    • Dose

  • Curna (Powder) : 0.5 - 1 g

  • Taila (Oil ) : 3 – 6 drops


    • Formulation:

  • Siitopaladi curna

  • Talisadya curna
